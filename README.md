Crow Canyon Software:

No-code and low-code business apps for Microsoft 365, Teams and SharePoint, all built on NITRO Studio.


Products:

NITRO Studio: no-code forms, workflows, dashboards and reports

NITRO Help Desk for IT: manage IT incidents and requests inside Microsoft 365 and Teams

NITRO Help Desk for HR: handle employee HR questions and requests in one place

NITRO Asset Management: track hardware, software and equipment, with check-in/check-out, license tracking and maintenance scheduling

NITRO Purchase Requests: submit, approve and track purchase requests and purchase orders

NITRO Request Manager: organize and fulfill routine requests with approvals

Contract Management: track contracts, licenses and subscriptions, with renewal alerts

Project Management: manage IT projects, with dashboards for progress, budget and tasks


Deployment supports SharePoint On-Premises, including connected and air-gapped environments, and SharePoint Online in commercial, GCC and GCC High environments.
