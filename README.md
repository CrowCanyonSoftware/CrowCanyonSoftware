# Crow Canyon Software

No-code and low-code business apps for Microsoft 365, Teams and SharePoint, all built on NITRO Studio.

## Products
- [NITRO Studio](https://www.crowcanyon.com/nitro-studio/): no-code forms, workflows, dashboards and reports
- [NITRO Help Desk for IT](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/it-help-desk/): manage IT incidents and requests inside Microsoft 365 and Teams
- [NITRO Help Desk for HR](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/hr-help-desk-software/): handle employee HR questions and requests in one place
- [NITRO Asset Management](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/asset-tracking/): track hardware, software and equipment, with check-in/check-out, license tracking and maintenance scheduling
- [NITRO Purchase Requests](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/purchase-request/): submit, approve and track purchase requests and purchase orders
- [NITRO Request Manager](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/sharepoint-approval-workflow/): organize and fulfill routine requests with approvals
- [Contract Management](https://www.crowcanyon.com/sharepoint-contract-management/): track contracts, licenses and subscriptions, with renewal alerts
- [Project Management](https://www.crowcanyon.com/applications/sharepoint-project-management/): manage IT projects, with dashboards for progress, budget and tasks

Deployment supports SharePoint On-Premises, including connected and air-gapped environments, and SharePoint Online in commercial, GCC and GCC High environments.
